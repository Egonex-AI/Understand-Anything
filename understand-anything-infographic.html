What it is
A multi-agent pipeline that turns a repo into a knowledge graph.
Tree-sitter parses source into structural facts (imports, exports, definitions, call sites). LLM agents supply what parsers cannot: plain-English summaries, architectural layers, business domains, guided tours. It all lands in .understand-anything/knowledge-graph.json, and a React dashboard makes it clickable. The graph is just JSON, so a team commits it once and everyone else skips the pipeline.
